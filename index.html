한글 → Han'gŭl

McCune-Reischauer with sound changes. Only Hangul is converted; English, numbers, emoji and punctuation stay as they are. Hover a word to find it on the other side.

Based on the converter at laotouzi.de/conv (lexilogos.com, adapted by laotouzi). Source and the original scripts: github.com/AARP41298/laotouzi2.
